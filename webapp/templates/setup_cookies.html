Why cookies, not OAuth?

Etsy's public API covers listings, orders, refunds, ads stats, etc. — that's the OAuth step you just did. But buyer messages, ad campaign create/edit, sales, coupons, and image reordering are not in the public API at all. The only way to read or change them is through etsy.com itself, which uses session cookies. Importing them from your own Chrome is the safest way: nothing leaves your machine.
